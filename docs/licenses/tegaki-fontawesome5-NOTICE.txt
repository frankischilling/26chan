Font Awesome Free icons used by the Tegaki icon font

Tegaki 0.9.4 includes Font Awesome Free's spray-can and pen-fancy icons as
custom Fontello glyphs named airbrush and pen. Their paths are rescaled from
512 to 1,000 units and rounded by the upstream Tegaki font configuration.

The upstream Tegaki configuration does not identify a Font Awesome release.
The icons were checked against Font Awesome Free 5.7.2, commit
1cf1062e0cfda5e9b5b2cac7844721b345e1a9c5:
https://github.com/FortAwesome/Font-Awesome/tree/1cf1062e0cfda5e9b5b2cac7844721b345e1a9c5

After rescaling, the spray-can and pen-fancy paths have the same SVG drawing
operations, with maximum coordinate differences below 0.14 and 0.05 units,
respectively. No glyph was changed by the 26chan asset extraction.
